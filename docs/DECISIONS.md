| D-039 | 2026-11-14 | Product card = one lazy chunk (`lib/card.ts`: Dialog, sonner, `ViewerCanvas`). Opens from the store (`cardOpen`); chunk is requested on hover/focus of "Explore the product" (skipped without WebGL), or on click. three/r3f/drei stay one shared chunk (`model-*.js`); the card chunks import it, nothing is duplicated (`WebGLRenderer` exists in one file only). `ViewerCanvas` uses `scene.clone()` of the cached `useGLTF` result (shared geometry/materials), so the page pouch is never re-parented; `pouch.glb` loads once. The page canvas runs `frameloop="demand"` while the card is open (0 draw calls measured). **No zoom** (`enableZoom={false}`): wheel and pinch keep scrolling the card. OrbitControls drives a hidden camera and the pouch is turned by its azimuth (orbiting the real camera left the fixed lights behind and the side went black). Canvas and r3f wrapper get `touch-action: pan-y`, so sideways drag turns and vertical swipe scrolls the card on phone. Idle for 2 s → pouch eases back to the front (instant with reduced motion). gzip: initial 132.9 KB, 3D 280.4 KB, card 29.2 KB, total 442.5 KB (budget 450). | Keeps first paint light; no second copy of three; touch scroll never captured by the card canvas. |

| D-042 | 2026-11-14 | One layout breakpoint: aspect ratio 1:1. Portrait (< 1): hero pouch in the top half (scale 0.9), text below, scroll hint at the bottom; story callouts below the pouch. Same rule in CSS (`wide:` variant) and in `Pouch.tsx` (positions as a share of the view). Reduced motion keeps the hero layout (still pouch). `tests/pouch-overlap.js` projects the pouch box to the screen (`?debug=pouch` sets `data-pouch-box` on the canvas) and asserts no visible text or image overlaps it, at the hero, each story step and the end, at 1440×900 and 390×844 (14 cases, 0 overlaps; proven to fail when a callout is pushed onto the pouch). The file is run with the Playwright MCP, not formatted by Biome (it adds a `;` the runner rejects); Phase 5 turns it into a real test. | Width alone broke on tablets (side callouts over a centred pouch). A measured check beats eyeballing. |
